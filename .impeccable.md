## Design Context

### Users

Remember Us serves two closely connected audiences: family caregivers who maintain trusted information and respond to reminders, and older adults living with early-to-moderate Alzheimer's disease who need a calm, simplified companion. The product is demonstrated across two phones linked by a six-digit family code. Family members configure profiles, calls, facts, and safety guidance; the elder-facing device prioritizes recognition, short answers, and step-by-step help returning home. Development and demonstrations use fictional data only.

### Brand Personality

Gentle, reassuring, and human. The interface should reduce anxiety, make the next action obvious, and feel like a caring household object rather than clinical software. Copy is concise and respectful, never childish or alarmist. The product name is always “Remember Us,” including in Chinese mode.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use a warm, organic, light-mode visual language with cream surfaces, terracotta accents, muted green support colors, softly illustrated family imagery, and the existing Baloo 2 / Noto Sans SC typography. Favor generous rounded forms and tactile controls while avoiding sterile hospital styling, generic SaaS dashboards, neon technology aesthetics, excessive glass effects, or decorative complexity. Short pages should use the full phone canvas with balanced vertical rhythm; long pages should scroll naturally from the top.

### Design Principles

1. Make the primary action and current state understandable within two seconds.
2. Preserve dignity: use plain, warm language and avoid infantilizing older adults.
3. Design elder-facing controls for reduced dexterity and cognition with large type, at least 44×44 px touch targets, strong focus states, and minimal choices.
4. Use space deliberately: group related content tightly, separate stages generously, and balance short screens vertically instead of leaving unused lower space.
5. Keep family and elder experiences visually related but context-appropriate, and preserve all critical functions across mobile, tablet, portrait, and landscape layouts.
